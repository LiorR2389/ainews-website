💳 Starting in October, banks in Cyprus will implement a recipient verification process for payments. Now, when making transfers, the system will automatically check the recipient's name against the IBAN to reduce errors and protect clients from fraud. If the name matches, the transfer will proceed without delays; in case of a typo, the system will issue a warning, and in the event of a complete mismatch, it will notify about the risk and may halt the transaction. The verification will take just a few seconds and will not require any additional actions from the client. This new system will become mandatory in all SEPA countries and will provide more confidence for residents of the Republic of Cyprus when making payments. @cyprus_control
