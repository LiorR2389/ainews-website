🪪 The government of Cyprus has officially abolished the provisions of the "golden passports" law. The investment program in exchange for citizenship, which was in effect from 2007 to 2020, is now permanently closed. The amendments stipulate that any new schemes for obtaining citizenship through investment can only be introduced by a separate law. Individuals who lost their passports under the previous program have a 60-day period to appeal the decision to an independent committee, and each revocation will be published in the official newspaper.
