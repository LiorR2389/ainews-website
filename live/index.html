➕ In the Republic of Cyprus, the rules for disciplinary control of doctors have been updated for the first time in 58 years. Among the changes, there is a provision for revoking the right to practice for serious violations and increasing fines for working without a license to 5,000 euros. The medical association insists on further strengthening control, but lawyers warn of risks to constitutional guarantees. @cyprus_control
