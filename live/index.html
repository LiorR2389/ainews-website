🪪 The government of Cyprus has officially abolished the provisions of the "golden passports" law. The investment program in exchange for citizenship, which operated from 2007 to 2020, is now definitively closed. The amendments stipulate that any new schemes for obtaining citizenship through investment can only be introduced by a separate law. For individuals who lost their passports under the previous program, there is a 60-day period to appeal the decision to an independent committee, and each revocation will be published in the official newspaper. The changes adopted are a response to criticism from the EU and aim to restore Cyprus's international reputation. @cyprus_control
